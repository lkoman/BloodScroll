BLOODSCROLL

https://lakoman.itch.io/blood-scroll

Every screen is a layer of hell, and the only way out is up.

BloodScroll is an endless vertical platformer. Each layer throws a wave of monsters at you: bats, spiders, slimes, jellyfish and worse. You choose whether to fight them.

But skipping them has a cost. Climb past a layer before you clear it and everything you left alive follows you up. Run far enough and you're carrying a swarm.

Every few layers a boss is waiting. Beat it and you get a gift: more max HP, a new gun, a shield, a double jump, a sword, life steal, and more.

Clear all five bosses and the loop starts over. The bosses come back harder and you keep building your character. How far can you get?

FEATURES

Endless climbing: no end screen, only a higher score
Mob waves follow you if you skip them
Five bosses per loop, each one tougher every time you meet it again
Boss gifts that keep stacking, so every run plays out differently
Seeded runs: share a seed and race a friend on the same climb
Guns, a sword, dashes and flower bombs
It's hard on purpose. Expect to die a lot.

CONTROLS

A / D or arrow keys: move
Space: jump (double jump once you unlock it)
Shift: sprint
Q / E: dash left / right
Left mouse: shoot
Right mouse: sword swing (once unlocked)
G or mouse wheel: switch weapon
F: pick up a flower / put down a bomb
Esc: pause
Default keys. Most keyboard controls can be rebound in Settings → Controls.
